The Outfit Formulas Guide

Most well-dressed people repeat a handful of combinations. Learn the formulas, then make them yours with colour and texture.

Why formulas work

An outfit formula is a tested recipe: a top, a bottom, a layer and a shoe that work together in shape and formality. Once you know it, getting dressed becomes a matter of choosing colours, not starting from nothing each morning.

Make it yours with colour

Pick one base colour (navy, black, grey or brown) and build most formulas in it. Add one or two accent colours that suit your skin tone. A formula in tonal shades of one colour looks especially polished.

Change the texture, not the shape

Swap a cotton T-shirt for a fine knit, denim for wool, or canvas trainers for leather. The formula stays the same, but the outfit moves from casual to smart.

The one-accessory rule

Choose one point of interest: a bold bag, interesting shoes, statement earrings or a patterned scarf. One strong piece looks intentional; four look like a costume.

Write your own list

Note the outfits you feel best in for two weeks. You will usually find three or four repeating formulas. Keep them on your phone, and try the Outfit Formula Builder for new ideas.
